## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an urgent, protective, yet reassuring interface engineered for urban households navigating hazardous seasonal smog and severe air quality crises. The aesthetic fuses atmospheric dark-mode immersion with clinical precision: high-contrast legibility, tactical clarity, and calm guidance under distressing environmental conditions.

The visual style blends **Atmospheric Dark Glassmorphism** with **Technical Precision**:
- **Atmospheric & Protective:** Deep nocturnal slate surfaces evoke indoor sanctuary and clean-air filtration, framing outside pollution hazards with uncompromising contrast.
- **Urgent Clarity:** Standardized CPCB (Central Pollution Control Board) air quality indicators serve as high-signal visual alerts without causing panic, balanced by optimistic cyan/teal clean-air accents.
- **Controlled Tactility:** Translucent card surfaces with razor-thin structural borders and soft atmospheric glow effects reinforce air flow, purification stages, and micro-climate awareness.

## Colors

The palette establishes an uncompromising atmospheric hierarchy built on an ink-slate backdrop, accented by the statutory CPCB National Air Quality Index scale and a forward-looking cyan/teal clean-air spectrum.

### Base Canvases & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#090D16` (Deep atmospheric nocturnal ground)
- **Card Surface:** `#131B2E` (Semi-translucent container layer)
- **Elevated Surface / Popover:** `#1E293B` (Floating dialogs, bottom sheets, sticky selectors)
- **Structural Border:** `#2A364F` (Subtle boundary stroke across containers)
- **High-Contrast Text Primary:** `#F8FAFC`
- **Muted Text Secondary:** `#94A3B8`
- **Subdued Text Tertiary:** `#64748B`

### Brand & Vision Accents
- **Primary Brand (Clean Air / Active):** `#06B6D4` (Teal-Cyan)
- **Secondary Brand (Vapor / Breeze):** `#38BDF8` (Sky Teal)

### Statutory CPCB AQI Semantic Spectrum
- **Good (0–50):** `#10B981` (Emerald)
- **Satisfactory (51–100):** `#84CC16` (Lime)
- **Moderate (101–200):** `#F59E0B` (Amber)
- **Poor (201–300):** `#F97316` (Orange)
- **Very Poor (301–400):** `#EF4444` (Red)
- **Severe (401–500+):** `#991B1B` (Deep Maroon/Crimson) with `#7F1D1D` containment backdrops.

## Typography

The type system pairs the geometric clarity and humanist balance of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for numerical metric readouts and atmospheric headlines with the rigorous utilitarian legibility of **Inter** for guidance instructions, body text, and micro-labels.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Numeric Metric & Display Hero:** Used exclusively for instantaneous AQI readings, indoor PM2.5 levels, and urgent weather advisories. Designed to remain crisp and unyielding at high visual densities.
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provide warm, modern structural anchors for cards, advisory sections, and multi-room air monitor switchers.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** High x-height ensures immediate comprehension of medical warnings (masks, air purifiers, outdoor exercise thresholds) on low-brightness mobile screens at dawn or during dense smog cover.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a responsive fluid grid system designed for immediate, glanceable triage on mobile devices while expanding into a modular dashboard on tablets and desktops.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (360px – 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). AQI metrics and family health alerts stack vertically in primary view.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid layout with `margin: 1.5rem` (24px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Dual-column comparison: Outdoor Neighborhood AQI paired with Indoor Air Purifier telemetry.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at max-width 1280px with `margin: 2.5rem` (40px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Spans multi-zone floor monitoring, 24-hour predictive forecast arcs, and vulnerability filters.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro-Gaps (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Sensor pill chips, status indicator dots, badge padding, metric sub-labels.
- **Container Insets (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Internal padding for air quality cards, danger alerts, and device control widgets.
- **Section Separators (`space-xl`, `space-2xl`):** Gap between real-time telemetry panels, advisory action blocks, and historical trend charts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows, which muddy dark interfaces. Instead, the design system utilizes **Translucent Tonal Layering, Glassmorphism, and Atmospheric Radial Glows**.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground (Canvas):** Base background `#090D16` absorbs ambient contrast, serving as a dark thermal void.
- **Surface Level 1 (Glassmorphic Cards):** `#131B2E` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined with a razor-thin 1px border of `#2A364F` (increased to 20% opacity white `#FFFFFF1A` at top border edge to emulate directional rim lighting).
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Popovers & Action Sheets):** Solid `#1E293B` with border `#334155`. Soft ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Status Luminescence (Atmospheric Glow):** In place of elevation drops, extreme threat statuses cast a diffused color radial haze:
  - Severe AQI generates a soft underglow: `box-shadow: 0 0 40px -10px rgba(153, 27, 27, 0.35)`.
  - Clean/Safe zones emit a soothing cyan-emerald halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 32px -8px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`)** with selected components deliberately transitioning into complete pill geometries (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to communicate biological safety, gentle airflow, and non-aggressive protection.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Standard Cards & Modules:** 0.5rem (8px) on mobile scaling up to 1rem (16px) on desktop via `rounded-lg` tokens.
- **Telemetry Indicators & Badges:** Full pill-shape (`9999px`) for AQI status tags (e.g., "Severe - 432", "Indoor Clean - 24"), status pills, and toggle switches.
- **Action Buttons & Form Controls:** Smooth 0.5rem (8px) radius providing firm ergonomic thumb anchors.
- **Progress Trackers & Gauges:** Pill-capped continuous bars with rounded edge terminals to depict continuous air streams.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Purify / Safe Mode):** Background in `#06B6D4` with bold dark text (`#090D16`). Hover transitions to `#38BDF8` with a subtle `0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)` glow.
- **Secondary (Explore / Details):** Translucent background `#1E293B`, border 1px solid `#2A364F`, text `#F8FAFC`.
- **Emergency Alert Action:** `#991B1B` background, `#F8FAFC` text, targeted for critical smog alerts and window sealing triggers.

### AQI Pill Chips
- **Structure:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally.
- **Status Mapping:** Background tint at 15% opacity of the respective CPCB color, coupled with a 1px solid border at 40% opacity, enclosing high-contrast bold white/status text and a 6px glowing indicator dot.

### Cards & Atmospheric Gauges
- **Glassmorphic Data Cards:** Built upon `#131B2E` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and 1px `#2A364F` border.
- **Dynamic AQI Hero Card:** Adapts its top rim border color to the current ambient air condition (e.g., `#EF4444` when outdoor air hits "Very Poor"), casting an inner gradient wash from top to bottom.

### Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** `#0E1524` with 1px border in `#2A364F`, switching to `#06B6D4` on focus with a 2px outer cyan ring (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`).
- **Text:** Input text in `#F8FAFC`, placeholder text in `#64748B`.

### Lists & Family Member Risk Rows
- **List Items:** Alternating soft surface highlights on hover (`#1E293B` at 50% opacity). Divided by 1px subtle rules of `#1A2438`.
- **Vulnerability Tags:** High-contrast sub-pills identifying sensitive groups (e.g., "Elderly", "Child with Asthma") with clear advisory text.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Toggles:** Pill-shaped track in `#1E293B` with border `#2A364F`. Active state fills with `#06B6D4`, shifting the thumb in pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checkboxes:** Squared 0.25rem (4px) rounded corners, filled with `#06B6D4` upon selection.
